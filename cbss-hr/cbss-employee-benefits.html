Employee Benefits Package

Welcome to the team. At CBSS we take care of the people who take care of our customers. The package below is the standard company offering: health coverage, retirement, ownership, faster pay, and travel and everyday rewards. Plan booklets and official documents always control the details.

Health & Everyday Protection

Medical

Company insurance

CBSS provides medical insurance so you and your eligible family members have a clear path to routine care and major medical needs.

  • Employee medical coverage through the company plan
  • Dependent coverage available at enrollment
  • In-network care and preventive visits as the plan allows
Vision & Dental

Vision and dental

Standard vision and dental coverage sits alongside medical so annual checkups, glasses, and dental work are part of the package — not an afterthought.

  • Vision exams, lenses, and frames per plan allowances
  • Dental preventive, basic, and major services per the schedule
  • Family enrollment during open or new-hire enrollment
Tax-advantaged

HSA

A Health Savings Account lets you set aside pre-tax dollars for qualified medical expenses. Unused funds stay with you and can grow over time.

  • Pre-tax contributions when you are in an HSA-eligible medical plan
  • Use for deductibles, prescriptions, and other IRS-qualified costs
  • Your account is portable if you change roles later
Tax-advantaged

FSA

A Flexible Spending Account is a simple way to pay eligible health or dependent-care costs with pre-tax pay.

  • Health care FSA for qualified out-of-pocket expenses
  • Dependent care FSA where offered for eligible childcare costs
  • Elect during enrollment; use funds in the plan year as the rules allow

Retirement & Ownership

401(k)

Company 401(k) with a 3% match

CBSS offers a traditional 401(k) so you can invest for retirement directly from payroll. The company match is straightforward:

  • 3% company match: CBSS matches 100% of the first 3% of eligible pay you contribute.
  • Example: if you earn $50,000 and contribute at least 3% ($1,500), the company adds $1,500.
  • Contributions are pre-tax, which can lower current taxable income.
  • You choose your contribution rate and investment mix from the plan menu.
  • Enrollment, eligibility, and vesting follow the official 401(k) plan document.
  • Loans, hardship withdrawals, and rollovers are available only as the plan and IRS rules allow.
Ownership

Company stock and crypto

Eligible team members may participate in CBSS ownership-style benefits. That includes company stock programs and company-sponsored crypto participation when those programs are offered for your role.

  • Company stock / equity participation as granted by leadership
  • Crypto / digital-asset benefits through approved company channels only
  • Awards, timing, and any holding rules are set in the grant or program paperwork

Pay, Banking & Everyday Rewards

Revolut partnership

Revolut benefits and same-day pay

CBSS partners with Revolut so you can get paid faster and manage money on a modern platform.

  • Same-day pay so earned wages can hit your account the same day, when you opt in
  • Revolut account and card tools for spending, transfers, and balances
  • Faster access to your pay versus a traditional wait-until-Friday cycle
  • Standard banking protections and program terms apply through Revolut
Services & rewards

Discount Tire

Team members receive services and rewards through Discount Tire — a practical benefit for the miles we put on the road.

  • Employee pricing and reward offers on tires and related services
  • Access through the CBSS / Discount Tire partner channel
  • Present your employee offer at participating Discount Tire locations
