Unique leadership opportunities

Advancement

Leadership is a paid path, not a title you wait on

CBSS runs unique internal leadership opportunities so high performers can step into larger seats — sales leadership, operations leadership, and company-level responsibility — without leaving the organization to get ahead. These are structured contests and advancement windows, reviewed by ownership. Winners do not just get a new name on a card. They contest, and unlock, bigger payment options, stronger 401(k) options, and improved benefits.

Leadership contests

Win the contest. Upgrade the package.

When you compete in a CBSS leadership contest and place, the company can move you onto an improved rewards tier. That is how we keep our best people inside the building.

Bigger payment options Higher leadership pay bands, contest bonuses, and expanded commission or override structures reserved for contest winners and promoted leaders.
Stronger 401(k) options Contest and leadership advancement can unlock improved 401(k) treatment above the standard 3% company match — including enhanced match opportunities and expanded contribution options as the plan allows.
Improved benefits Upgraded medical, vision, and dental elections, larger HSA / FSA room where eligible, priority travel, and increased company stock / crypto participation.

Standard benefits below are the company floor. Leadership contests are how the ceiling moves. Official contest rules, plan documents, and grant paperwork set the exact numbers for each cycle.

Retirement, match, and ownership

401(k)

Company 401(k) — 3% match, with contest upgrades

CBSS offers a traditional 401(k) so retirement is funded from payroll, the same way a large American company does it. The standard match is simple. Leadership contests can improve it.

  • Standard company match: CBSS matches 100% of the first 3% of eligible pay you contribute.
  • Example: on $50,000 of eligible pay, a 3% deferral is $1,500 and the company adds $1,500.
  • Leadership / contest 401(k) options: placing in a leadership contest, or moving into a leadership seat, can unlock improved 401(k) options — a richer match opportunity, additional contribution room, or other plan features as that year’s program and the plan document allow.
  • Contributions are pre-tax and can lower current taxable income.
  • You choose your deferral rate and the investment mix from the plan menu.
  • Enrollment, eligibility, vesting, loans, hardships, and rollovers follow the official 401(k) plan and IRS rules.
