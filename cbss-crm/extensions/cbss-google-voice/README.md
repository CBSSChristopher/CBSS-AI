# CBSS Google Voice helper

Chrome helper for the CBSS CRM. It does not log into Google for you. It finds the Google Voice tab you already have open and sends Call / Text there.

## Install once

1. Download `gv-helper.zip` from the CRM Google Voice panel.
2. Unzip it.
3. Open `chrome://extensions`.
4. Turn on Developer mode.
5. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder (the one with `manifest.json`).
6. Sign in to [Google Voice](https://voice.google.com) with your `@cbshippingsolutions.com` Google account. Keep that tab open.

Call and Text in the CRM then reuse that tab instead of opening a new one for every contact.
