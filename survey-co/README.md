# survey-co

Research pack for a new Bay Area land surveying firm. The brand is not chosen; the notes say [Survey Co].

This folder is meant to move to its own repository as-is, for example:

```
git subtree split --prefix=survey-co -b survey-co-export
```

Nothing in this folder imports from or depends on the repository it currently sits in.

## Contents

- `competitors/bay_area_competitors.csv` — 45 firms that serve the nine-county Bay Area (Santa Clara stops at Gilroy). Blank cells were not observed. Research date is November 10, 2026.
- `competitors/report.md` — takeaways, cited price ranges, gaps in the online experience, what to copy from the stronger marketers, and positioning notes.
- `competitors/screenshots/` — desktop captures of stronger and weaker sites, plus the Google block page, Yelp block page, and Meta Ad Library screens from the same day.
- `brief/surveying_brief.md` — client-facing services, cited prices and turnaround, the quote-form questions, the counties and cities, and a licensing section. That licensing section is operations background. It is not required on the website for now.

Prices are copied from the sources named in the brief. They are not a fee schedule.
