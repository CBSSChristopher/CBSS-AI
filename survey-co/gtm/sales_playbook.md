# [Survey Co] sales playbook

Prepared November 10, 2026. This is the sales process for a new Bay Area land surveying firm. The quote form, partner page, and CRM are being built separately. Build them to match this playbook.

The service area is the nine counties in `customer_segments.md`. Gilroy is the southern edge. Habu is an owner and a referral source. Individuals such as Luiza use the same partner path as any other referrer. There is no separate presentation track.

## Who does what

| Person | Does | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Christopher, operations lead | Alerts on every new request. Calls, texts, and emails clients and partners. Qualifies, quotes, collects the deposit, schedules field work, delivers the map, asks for the review. | Hand a new lead to Travis. |
| Joe, chief of operations | The same client and partner communication. Owns the field calendar, the work order, county filing clocks, and capacity. | Hand a new lead to Travis. |
| Travis, licensed land surveyor | Field work. Technical production under his license, including responsible charge and the stamp on work he is responsible for. | CRM updates, lead alerts, sales calls, quote follow-up, client or partner texts, emails, or review asks. He is not scheduled by the client. Joe or Christopher schedule him. |
| AI assistant | Writes the automated receipt, drafts replies, files the lead, alerts Christopher and Joe, reminds them of follow-ups, stops the marketing drip when a quote is active. | Mark a lead “answered.” Send a client or partner email in the first 90 days unless Christopher or Joe turned that template on. Alert Travis. |

If both Christopher and Joe are out, they cover each other. Travis is not the backup on the phone.

Business hours for the response clock: 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific, Monday through Friday, excluding days the firm posts as closed. Christopher or Joe can change the clock. They cannot change the rule that a person answers.

## CRM stages

Use these stage names exactly.

| Stage | Meaning | Who moves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New request | A form, call, text, email, or partner referral has a property or a person, and no one has qualified it. | System, when the lead is created. |
| Qualified | The parcel is inside the service area, the product is something the firm does, and a person has spoken with the client or the authorized partner. | Christopher or Joe. |
| Site research | Record maps, easements, and access are being checked so the quote matches the parcel. | Christopher or Joe. Travis is not assigned a research task in the CRM. |
| Quote sent | A written scope, fee, and deposit terms are in the client’s hands. | Christopher or Joe. |
| Deposit paid | The starting payment has cleared. The job is allowed onto the field calendar. | Christopher or Joe. |
| Scheduled | A field day is on Joe or Christopher’s calendar, and the client knows the window. | Christopher or Joe. |
| Field complete | The field work Joe or Christopher ordered is back. | Christopher or Joe, when the work returns. Travis does not move the stage. |
| Drafting | The map or exhibit is being prepared for the licensee’s review and stamp. | Christopher or Joe. |
| Delivered/recorded | The client has the deliverable. If a Record of Survey or corner record had to be filed, the filing is done or the county has it and the client has been told the status. | Christopher or Joe. |
| Closed lost | The firm or the client stopped. A reason is required. | Christopher or Joe. |

Closed-lost reasons, pick one: out of area, no survey needed, price, timing, no response, chose another firm, conflict, capacity, do not contact.

## Speed to lead

**Operating target:** Christopher or Joe send a human reply within **one business hour** of a new request. The clock uses the business hours above. A request at 7:00 p.m. is due by 9:00 a.m. the next business day. A request at 4:30 p.m. is due by 5:00 p.m. that day only if an hour remains; otherwise it is due in the first hour of the next business morning. Do not split the hour across two days. If less than one hour remains, the reply is due in the first hour of the next business day.

The automated receipt is not the human reply. It goes out within a few minutes so the person knows the form worked.

**Receipt text, email and text:**

> [Survey Co] received your request for [address or “a survey”]. Christopher or Joe will reply within one business hour (weekdays, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific). If this is urgent, reply with the date you are up against.

Alert Christopher and Joe on every new request. The alert includes name, phone, email, address, city, source, partner name if any, and the one-hour deadline. Do not include Travis on the alert, the task, or the inbox rule.

A missed hour is logged as a miss. It is reviewed in the Monday meeting. It is not a reason to add Travis to the rotation.

## Inbound quote request

### Call script

Christopher or Joe. If the AI assistant answers a live call in some future setup, it still only takes a message and alerts them. It does not quote.

> This is [Christopher / Joe] at [Survey Co]. I have your note about [address]. I want to make sure we send the right kind of survey, or tell you if you do not need one.
>
> What are you trying to do: a fence, an ADU or addition, a lot split, a sale or a title issue, or something else?
>
> Is there a date you care about, a permit, a closing, or a contractor?
>
> Has the property been surveyed before, and do you have a map?
>
> I will look at the recorded maps and send a written scope. I will not give you a firm fee on this call. If the parcel is outside our area, I will say so now.

Then qualify with the checklist. If they want a number immediately:

> A fee without the map in front of me is a guess, and guesses are how survey jobs go wrong. You will have a written number from us after the record check, or you will have a note that says we should not take it.

### Text script

> [Survey Co]: this is [Christopher / Joe]. We have [address]. What are you trying to settle: fence, ADU, lot split, or a title issue? Any deadline? I will reply with the next step today.

### Email script

Subject: Survey request for [street, city]

> [Name], thank you for the request on [address].
>
> To scope it I need: what you are trying to do, any deadline, and any earlier map or title report you already have. A reply to this email is enough.
>
> We survey in the nine Bay Area counties, south through Gilroy. If this parcel is outside that, I will tell you and I will not keep the file open.
>
> [Christopher / Joe]
> [Survey Co]
> [phone]

Attach nothing that looks like a quote. Do not copy Travis.

## Outbound

Outbound is a conversation Christopher or Joe start. Twenty minutes of preparation, then a short note. No purchased blast list in the first 90 days.

### Real estate agents

Subject: When a seller cannot say where the fence is

> [Name], I am [Christopher / Joe] at [Survey Co]. We are a new Bay Area land surveying firm. Habu is one of our owners and sends us properties when a report raises a line, a fence, or a lot-split question.
>
> When you are on a listing and the seller cannot say whether the fence or an addition is on the line, send the address through our partner form: [URL]. We reply to your client within one business hour on business days, and we write you the same day to say we have it. We do not pay a referral fee. We do tell you what happened, without the price, unless your client wants you to see it.
>
> If a ten-minute call is useful, reply with a time.
>
> [Christopher / Joe]

### Title officers

Subject: 2026 ALTA surveys, one crew, Bay Area only

> [Name], I am [Christopher / Joe] at [Survey Co]. We prepare ALTA/NSPS land title surveys to the standards that took effect February 23, 2026.
>
> We are not asking the insurer to pay the survey. The standards put that cost on the client unless the insurer agrees in writing. What we can do is turn a commitment and a Table A list around with a written scope, and keep you posted when the plat is out.
>
> The partner form is [URL]. Commercial files inside the nine counties, through Gilroy. If a residential escrow does not actually need a survey, we would rather say so.
>
> [Christopher / Joe]

### Architects

Subject: Site surveys for ADUs and additions

> [Name], I am [Christopher / Joe] at [Survey Co], a Bay Area land surveying firm. When you need a boundary and topographic map before you place an ADU, an addition, or a new house, send the address and the file format you want: [URL].
>
> You will hear from Christopher or Joe, not from a call center. Field work is scheduled only after the client has accepted a written scope. Tell us the layer names and the vertical datum you prefer and we will keep them on your partner record.
>
> [Christopher / Joe]

Leave cities, utilities, and lenders off the outbound list for 90 days. Lenders come with the title officer. The workload cap in the marketing plan is 15 new partner conversations in 90 days. That cap includes these notes.

## Qualification checklist

Christopher or Joe complete this before moving a lead to Qualified. If an item is unknown, the lead stays in New request and the missing item is the next question.

1. Parcel address, city, county, and assessor’s parcel number if the client has it.
2. Inside the nine counties, and not south of Gilroy. San Benito, Santa Cruz, Monterey, San Joaquin, and Sacramento are a polite no.
3. What they want to do, in their words, and which product it sounds like: fence or boundary, corner record, Record of Survey, topo, ADU combo, SB 9, ALTA, staking, elevation certificate, lot-line adjustment, parcel map, dispute exhibit.
4. Who pays, and whether that person knows a surveyor will contact them.
5. Deadline: permit, closing, contractor, hearing, or none.
6. Earlier surveys, title report, or Habu report in hand.
7. Access: owner-occupied, tenant, gate, dog, hillside, locked site. Who Christopher or Joe should call. Not who Travis should call.
8. Is there a dispute, an attorney, or a neighbor who has already hired a surveyor? If yes, stop and use the conflict check before promising a site visit.
9. Commercial or residential. If commercial and ALTA, ask whether a current title commitment and a Table A list exist.
10. The client can pay a deposit before field work and the balance before the stamped deliverable is released. If they cannot, it is not Qualified yet. It may be Closed lost, price or timing.

Out-of-area reply:

> Thank you for thinking of us. [Address] is outside the area we survey. We work the nine Bay Area counties and stop at Gilroy, so we should not take this one. A surveyor who practices in [county] is the right call.

Do not invent a referral firm. Offer no name unless Christopher or Joe personally know a licensee who has agreed to receive overflow, and that agreement is written down later. It is not assumed here.

## Site research and the quote

Move to Site research only after qualification. Christopher or Joe pull the recorded map, a current assessor description, and any easements the client already has. The quote states:

- The product and what is not included.
- Whether a Record of Survey or a corner record is expected, and that section 8762 can require a Record of Survey after the field work even if the quote hoped otherwise. Say that in plain language.
- County fees as a pass-through, from that county’s current schedule. The Santa Clara County surveyor schedule for FY 2026–2027 lists a Record of Survey review minimum of $1,299 plus a 3% technology fee, and a $15 corner-record filing fee. Confirm the adopted schedule before using the number. Do not use it for any other county. Source in `customer_segments.md`.
- Professional fee, deposit, balance, and what “delivered” means.
- How long the quote is good. **Operating choice:** 30 days, then Christopher or Joe re-check the calendar.
- The one-crew limit in one sentence if the start date is more than a short wait.

**Operating target:** a standard residential quote leaves within two business days of Qualified. An ALTA quote leaves within five business days of Qualified, and only once the commitment or a written Table A list is in hand. If the client cannot get the commitment, send a fee range labeled as a range and do not call it a quote. Those targets are internal. They are not a promise in the ad.

Joe decides whether the field date in the quote is one the crew can keep. Travis is asked about technical duration by Joe, outside the CRM alert. The client-facing date comes from Joe or Christopher.

## Quote follow-up

The marketing nurture stops while this cadence is running.

| When | What Christopher or Joe do |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Send the quote. Email plus a short text: “Quote for [street] is in your email. Happy to walk through it.” |
| Business day 2 | One phone call. If no answer, one text. |
| Business day 5 | Email: one question, usually the deadline or the scope, not a discount. |
| Business day 10 | Email: the quote expires on [date]. Ask if they want it renewed. |
| Day 30, or the expiry date | If there is no answer, move to Closed lost, reason no response, and send one line that the file is closed and they can reopen it. |

Stop the cadence the moment they reply, accept, or say no. A no is Closed lost the same day, with the reason they gave.

Do not discount on day 5 to create a reply. If the scope was wrong, revise the quote and restart the clock. Discounting an unchanged scope trains the next client to wait.

## Objections

### Price

> The number has four parts: record research, the field visit, the map, and any county filing fee. The county fee is set by the county, and we pass it through. On a Santa Clara County Record of Survey the county’s own review minimum is $1,299 before the technology fee, which is why a “corner check” and a Record of Survey are not the same product. If you only need the corners marked and the statute does not force a Record of Survey, the scope should say that. I would rather shrink the scope than pretend the county fee is optional.

Do not invent a cheaper product that still has to be filed. If section 8762 applies, say so.

### Timing

> We will not put a field day on the calendar until the deposit is in, because that day is the only crew we have. If your permit or closing is before we can honestly be there, I will tell you now. What I can do today is finish the record check and hold a date once the deposit clears. I cannot skip the research to look faster.

### “My neighbor already had a survey”

> Your neighbor’s survey marks their work on their parcel. It is useful research. It does not move your corners, it does not put a map in your name, and it does not satisfy your lender, your designer, or a fence you are about to build. If their surveyor set a monument on the line we share, we will look for it and we will show it. You still need your own survey if you need your line marked or a map you can hand to the city.

### “The title company did not ask for one”

> In a California residential sale that is common. The policy often keeps the survey exception. You need a survey when you are building, fencing, splitting, or resolving a line, not because every escrow orders one. If a title officer does want survey coverage changed, we will talk to them with your permission and scope an ALTA or a smaller exhibit to match the exception.

### “Can you just use the satellite image”

> An image does not establish a property corner. We use recorded maps, monuments in the ground, and measurements. If an image were enough, we would say you do not need us.

### “Can Travis call me”

> Travis is our field surveyor. Scheduling and questions come from Christopher or Joe so the answers stay consistent and the field day stays intact. We will pass along access instructions ourselves.

## Deposit and scheduling

The dollar amount or percentage of the deposit is not set in this playbook. No sourced Bay Area fee study was used to pick one. Christopher and Joe lock the number in the quote template before the first quote goes out, and they use that number until they deliberately change it.

Rules that are set:

- No field date until the deposit has cleared.
- No release of the stamped deliverable until the balance has cleared, unless Christopher or Joe make a written exception on that job.
- The client receives a written scope before paying. A card authorization on the website is acceptable if it stores the same scope and the same amount.
- Christopher or Joe, not the client and not Travis, place the job on the calendar.

Scheduling steps:

1. Deposit clears. Stage becomes Deposit paid.
2. Christopher or Joe call the client for access: gate code, dog, tenant, parking, and whether someone must be home. They write those facts on the work order.
3. Joe, or Christopher if Joe is out, assigns the field day and moves the stage to Scheduled. They tell the client the window.
4. The work order Travis receives contains address, scope, what to measure, access notes, and safety notes. It is not a CRM login and not a client thread. Questions from the field go to Joe or Christopher, who call the client if something changes.
5. If the client must reschedule, they call Christopher or Joe. Travis does not renegotiate the day on site.
6. When the field work is back, Joe or Christopher move the stage to Field complete, then to Drafting while the map is prepared for review and stamp.
7. Business and Professions Code section 8762(c) gives 90 days after monuments are set, or after the field survey is complete, whichever is first, to file a required Record of Survey. Joe tracks that date. He coordinates the technical schedule so the licensee can meet it. The client is told if county review, not the firm, is what is taking the time.

811 / USA notification is the excavator’s call when someone is going to dig. A boundary survey that does not excavate does not need a sales step about 811. Construction staking near utilities does. Joe puts that on the work order when the scope is staking. Do not make it a slogan.

## Delivery, review, and the referral ask

Christopher or Joe send the deliverable. The email says what the client received, what was filed, and what the county still has to do if the map is in review.

Review ask, same day, separate short paragraph. It must match the review rules in the marketing plan. No gift, no discount, no “only if you are happy, click here.”

> If the work was what we said it would be, a Google review helps the next owner find a licensed surveyor: [direct link]. If something was not right, reply to this email and we will deal with it.

Referral ask, in the same email, not as a condition of the review:

> If an agent, architect, or neighbor is about to build a fence or an ADU and does not know where the line is, send them here: [partner form or quote form].

Day 7 reminder if no review and no complaint, one time, then stop.

Stage stays Delivered/recorded. Do not invent a later stage for “reviewed.”

## Referral partner program

Partners are real estate agents and brokers, title and escrow officers, Habu, and individuals such as Luiza. They all use one path. Habu’s ownership is recorded on the partner record so reports can separate owner referrals from outside referrals. It does not change the steps.

### Partners page and form

The site has a Partners page, linked from the footer. It contains a short explanation and a form. Suggested path: `/partners`.

The page says:

- Who may use it: agents, title and escrow, Habu, and any person sending a property with the owner’s knowledge.
- What happens next: Christopher or Joe contact the client within one business hour on business days, unless the partner marks “contact me first.”
- What the partner receives: a same-day thank-you and later status, not the fee, unless the client says the partner may see the fee.
- What the firm does not do in this launch: pay a cash referral fee or a percentage of the survey.

Form fields:

| Field | Required |
| --- | --- |
| Partner name | Yes |
| Partner type: Real estate agent, Title or escrow, Habu, Individual, Other | Yes |
| Company or affiliation | Yes for agent, title, and Habu. Optional for an individual. |
| Partner email and phone | Yes |
| Client name | Yes |
| Client phone and email | Yes, unless “contact me first” is checked |
| Property address and city | Yes |
| Assessor’s parcel number | No |
| What the partner thinks is needed | No, free text |
| Deadline | No |
| Contact me first, before you call the client | No, checkbox |
| The client expects [Survey Co] to contact them, or I am the client’s representative and I am asking you to contact them | Yes, one of those two |
| How the partner knows the client | No |

Spam and duplicate submissions are still created as leads, marked for review, and still alert Christopher and Joe. They are not auto-deleted.

### How the CRM tags and tracks the lead

On create:

- Stage: New request
- Lead source: Referral
- `partner_type` as chosen on the form
- `partner_name`, `partner_org`, `partner_email`, `partner_phone`
- `partner_record_id` linked to a partner record, created if it does not exist
- Tags: `referral` and the partner type (`agent`, `title`, `habu`, `individual`, or `other`)
- Habu submissions: `partner_org` = Habu, tag `habu`, and the partner record flagged owner-affiliate
- An individual such as Luiza: `partner_type` = Individual, `partner_name` as given, tag `individual`
- Store the form id and the time received
- Alert: Christopher and Joe only, with the one-hour deadline and the partner’s name
- No task, notification, or owner assigned to Travis

The partner record accumulates: referrals sent, quotes sent, deposits paid, delivered, closed lost, and the last thank-you date. Christopher or Joe can filter the pipeline by partner without exporting a spreadsheet.

If the same property arrives from the website and from a partner, keep one job, keep both sources, and give the partner credit on the referral fields. Do not make two quotes.

### Follow-up and thank-you

1. Automated receipt to the partner, immediately: “We have your referral for [city]. Christopher or Joe will take it from here.” This is not the human reply to the client.
2. Human reply to the client within one business hour, using the inbound scripts, unless the partner checked “contact me first.” In that case the human reply within one business hour goes to the partner, and the client is contacted only after the partner says to proceed.
3. Same business day, Christopher or Joe thank the partner. Email is enough. A text is optional. The note names the city and the street, not the fee.

> [Name], thank you for sending [street, city]. We have reached the client / we are waiting for your go-ahead, and we will tell you when a quote goes out. We will not include the fee unless the client says we may.

4. At Quote sent: one status note to the partner. “The quote is with the client.” No dollar amount unless the client agreed.
5. At Deposit paid or Scheduled: one status note. “They are scheduled.” No need to give the field window unless the partner is coordinating access, and then only with the client’s agreement.
6. At Delivered/recorded: thank them again and invite the next property through the same form. No gift card.
7. At Closed lost: close the loop in one or two sentences (“They paused” or “They decided not to proceed”). Do not blame the client and do not disclose the price.

If a partner asks for a percentage, Christopher or Joe say the firm is not paying referral fees during the launch, and that any paid arrangement has to be reviewed by counsel first. Reasons to leave it with counsel rather than invent a rate: real-estate referral rules, insurance and settlement-service rules, and professional-conduct rules for licensed surveyors. Habu’s return is ownership, not a per-job bounty. Do not draft a fee schedule in a follow-up email.

A handwritten note after the first delivered referral from a given partner is appropriate. It is not required, and it is not a condition the partner is promised on the website.

## KPIs by stage

These are operating targets for a firm with no history. They are not industry conversion rates. Do not publish them as expected close rates. Review them every Monday.

| Stage | Target | Read it this way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New request | 100% get a human reply from Christopher or Joe within one business hour. Alerts that include Travis: 0. | A miss is logged. Two misses in a week means the coverage between Christopher and Joe is broken. |
| Qualified | Every open New request older than two business days has a stated missing fact or has been moved. | Speed of qualification, not a percentage of leads that “should” qualify. |
| Site research | Standard residential research is done inside the two-business-day quote target. ALTAs inside five, once the commitment is in hand. | If research slips, the quote target slips with it. Say so to the client. |
| Quote sent | 100% of Qualified jobs that the firm intends to price receive a written quote. 100% of those quotes have a follow-up task on day 2, day 5, day 10, and expiry. | Activity target. |
| Deposit paid | No Scheduled job without a cleared deposit, except a written exception on that job. | Binary. |
| Scheduled | The client was given the window by Christopher or Joe. The work order exists before Travis is on site. | Check ten jobs at random each month once volume exists. In the first month, check all of them. |
| Field complete | Stage moved by Christopher or Joe within one business day of the crew returning. | Measures handoff, not Travis’s paperwork. |
| Drafting | The 90-day filing clock, when it applies, has an owner (Joe) and a date on the job. | A job with monuments set and no date is a defect. |
| Delivered/recorded | 100% receive the review ask and the referral sentence the day the deliverable is sent. | Completeness, not a star goal. |
| Closed lost | 100% have a reason. Partner-sourced losses have a closed-loop note. | Otherwise the Monday report hides why work died. |

Weekly counts to read, without targets attached: new requests by source (search, profile, partner, Habu, individual, other), quotes sent, deposits, delivered, closed lost by reason, and median business hours to the human reply. After 30 days, add Search cost beside partner-sourced deposits. Not before. There is not enough data, and this plan did not assume a cost per lead.

## Monday meeting

Thirty minutes. Christopher and Joe. Agenda:

1. Any response older than one business hour, and why.
2. Quotes expiring in the next seven days.
3. Field days in the next two weeks, and any job that would overload them.
4. Filing clocks inside 30 days.
5. Partner thank-you notes owed.
6. Search terms and negatives, once ads are on.
7. One process fix, written down, or an explicit decision to change nothing.

Travis does not attend this meeting. Joe talks with him about field technique and schedule outside it.
